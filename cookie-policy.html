What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences (such as login details, language, font size, and other display preferences) over a period of time.

Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

1. Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

  • Purpose: To keep the website secure and functional.
  • Examples: Session cookies, CSRF tokens.

2. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us understand which pages are the most and least popular.

  • Purpose: To analyze website usage and improve user experience.
  • Examples: Google Analytics, Jetpack Stats.
  • Data Collected: Anonymous data such as page views, bounce rate, and referral sources.

3. Advertising & Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an ad and measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns.

  • Purpose: To display relevant ads and track ad performance.

Your Cookie Preferences – How to Control Them

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences through the following methods:

1. Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit getage247.xyz, you will see a cookie consent banner. You can choose to:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies, including analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Customize: Select which types of cookies you wish to enable.
  • Reject All (except essential): Only allow essential cookies.

🍪 We use cookies to enhance your experience. By continuing to visit this site, you agree to our use of cookies.

2. Browser Settings

You can also manage your cookie preferences directly through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies already stored on your device.
  • Block all cookies (though this may affect the functionality of some websites).
  • Set preferences for specific websites.

Here are links to the cookie settings for popular browsers:

4. Third-Party Opt-Out

You can also opt out of third-party cookies used for advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.
