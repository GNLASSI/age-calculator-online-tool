How to Calculate Your Age from CNIC Number in Pakistan

Your CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) contains your complete date of birth — encoded right inside the 13-digit number. If you have ever lost track of your exact birthdate, need to verify your age for a job form, or simply want to double-check what NADRA has on record, your CNIC number is the starting point.

This guide explains exactly how to read your date of birth from your CNIC number, how to calculate your exact age from it, and which free online tool does the calculation for you in under 5 seconds.

How Is a CNIC Number Structured?

A Pakistani CNIC number has 13 digits in this format:

XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X
Example: 35202-1234567-3

Here is what each part means:

PartDigitsMeaning
First 5 digits35202Province and district code
Next 7 digits1234567Unique registration number
Last 1 digit3Gender (odd = male, even = female)

The CNIC number itself does not directly contain your date of birth in the digits — unlike what many people believe. Your date of birth is stored separately in the NADRA database linked to your CNIC number, not embedded in the number itself. [Source: NADRA Pakistan]

So Where Is the Date of Birth on a CNIC?

Your date of birth is printed on the front face of your physical CNIC card. Look at the card and you will see:

  • Date of Birth: printed in DD.MM.YYYY format — for example, 15.03.1990
  • Date of Issue — when the card was issued
  • Date of Expiry — when the card expires (usually 10 years after issue)

Important: The date of birth on your CNIC is the official record held by NADRA. If you need to prove your age for any government, legal, or employment purpose in Pakistan, this is the date you should use.

How to Verify Your Date of Birth Using NADRA SMS Service

If you do not have your physical CNIC card with you, you can verify your date of birth using NADRA's official SMS service:

  1. Open your phone's messaging app.
  2. Type your 13-digit CNIC number (without dashes).
  3. Send the SMS to 8300.
  4. You will receive a reply with your basic NADRA record including your name and date of birth.

This service costs a standard SMS rate and works on all Pakistani mobile networks — Jazz, Zong, Telenor, and Ufone. [Source: NADRA Pakistan — CNIC Services]

Why Do People Need to Calculate Age from CNIC?

In Pakistan, age verification from CNIC comes up in many practical situations:

Government Job Applications

Almost every government job in Pakistan has an age limit. FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, and other commissions require candidates to be within a specific age range on the closing date of the application. Knowing your exact age — not just your birth year — is essential because being even one day over the age limit can disqualify you. [Source: FPSC Pakistan]

School and University Admissions

Many schools and universities in Pakistan require students to be within a certain age bracket. HEC (Higher Education Commission) and various provincial education departments set minimum and maximum age requirements for specific programs.

Passport Applications

When applying for a Pakistani passport, your age determines which category of passport fee and validity applies. Children under 18 receive a different passport type than adults.

Bank Account Opening

Pakistani banks require customers to be at least 18 years old to open an independent account. Age is verified against the CNIC date of birth.

Pension and Retirement Planning

Government employees need to know their exact age to calculate how many years of service remain before retirement at 60, and to plan pension contributions accordingly.

What If Your CNIC Date of Birth Is Wrong?

This is more common than people realize. If your CNIC shows an incorrect date of birth — perhaps entered wrongly during initial registration — you can get it corrected through NADRA:

  • Visit your nearest NADRA Registration Centre (NRC).
  • Bring original supporting documents: birth certificate, matriculation certificate, or Form-B (child registration certificate).
  • Submit a correction request. NADRA will verify the documents and update the record.
  • A correction fee applies and processing typically takes 7 to 30 working days.

It is important to fix this as early as possible, since all official records — job applications, passports, bank accounts — are linked to the date of birth on your CNIC. [Source: NADRA Pakistan]
