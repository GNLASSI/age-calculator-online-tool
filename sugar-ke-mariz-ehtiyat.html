Sugar ke Mariz ke Liye 10 Zaroori Ehtiyat - Diabetes Care Guide

Sughar aur Pakistan me har 4 me se 1 shakhs sugar ka mariz hai. Agar ehtiyat ki jaye to normal zindagi guzar sakte hain. Ye 10 baatein roz yaad rakhein:

1. Cheeni aur Meetha Band

Cheeni, mithai, cold drink, packaged juice bilkul band. Gud bhi nuksan deta hai. Chaye me "sugar free" istemal karein.

2. Roti ka Hisab Rakhein

1 waqt me 1 ya maximum 1.5 roti. Zyada roti = zyada sugar. Brown atta wali roti behtar hai.

3. Roz 30 Min Walk Zaroori

Khane ke baad 20-30 min walk kar lein. Insulin khud kaam karegi. Sughar ki gali me walk sabse best hai.

4. Paon ka Khas Khyal

Roz paon check karein. Koi zakham, kaanta, sozish ho to foran doctor ko dikhayein. Sugar me zakham dair se bharte hain.

5. Pani Zyada Piyein

Roz 8-10 glass pani. Sugar high ho to pyaas zyada lagti hai. Dehydration se bachein.

6. Stress se Bachein

Tension = Sugar high. Namaz, tilawat, walk se zehan relax rakhein.

7. Waqt pe Dawa/Insulin

Doctor ne jo time diya hai usi time dawa lein. 1 ghanta aage peeche na karein.

8. Phal Samajh ke Khayein

Kela, angoor, kharboza kam khayein. Amrood, apple, papita thora kha sakte hain. Phal bhi sugar barhata hai.

9. 3 Mahine baad Test

HbA1c test har 3 mahine baad karwayein. Is se pata chalta hai 3 mahine sugar control rahi ya nahi.

10. Emergency Kit Rakhein

Bag me hamesha toffee/biscuit rakhein. Agar sugar low ho jaye "chakkar" aaye to foran kha lein.

Zaroori Note: Ye tips sirf maloomat ke liye hain. Apne doctor ke mashware baghair dawa band na karein. Emergency me foran hospital jayein.
