📖 About This Tool

MetaGen is a powerful, free meta tag generator built for bloggers, SEO specialists, and web developers. It helps you create perfectly optimized meta tags for better search engine visibility and social sharing.

🔧 Features

  • Meta Tags: Title, Description, Keywords, Author, Robots, Canonical
  • Open Graph: OG Title, Description, Image, Type, URL, Site Name
  • Twitter Cards: Summary, Summary Large Image, App
  • Live Preview: See how your tags will look in search results
  • SEO Score: Get instant feedback on your meta tag quality
  • Copy & Download: Copy HTML to clipboard or download as .html
  • No API Required: Everything runs client-side — 100% free and private

🌐 Why Meta Tags Matter

Meta tags are the foundation of on-page SEO. They tell search engines what your page is about and control how your content appears in search results and social media shares. Well-crafted meta tags can significantly improve your click-through rates and organic traffic.
