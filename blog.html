7 Din Ka Weight Loss Diet Plan - Ghar Ka Khana Se 5kg Kam Karein

Weight loss karna mushkil nahi agar plan saaf ho. Ye 7 din ka diet plan bilkul ghar ke khane se bana hai. Bina mehnge supplements ke, bina gym ke. Sirf discipline chahiye.

Important: Pehle apna BMI check kar lein. Agar BMI 25+ hai to ye plan aap ke liye perfect hai. Free BMI Calculator Check Karein

7 Din Ka Diet Plan Table

Day 1: Detox Day

  • Subha khali pait: 1 glass garam pani + adha lemon + shahad
  • Nashta 8am: 2 boiled eggs + 1 slice brown bread + chai bina cheeni
  • Dopher 1pm: 1 roti + daal + salad kheera, tamatar
  • Sham 5pm: Green tea + 5 badam
  • Raat 8pm: Grilled chicken 100g + sabzi

Day 2 to Day 6: Fat Burn Days

Rozana roti 1 hi khayein. Chawal band. Cheeni, cold drinks bilkul band.

  • Subha: Oats ya 2 boiled eggs
  • Dopher: Salad + dahi + 1 roti + sabzi
  • Sham: Fruit - apple ya papita 1 bowl
  • Raat: Soup + boiled chicken ya machli

Day 7: Cheat Day Light

1 time apna pasand ka khana kha sakte hain, lekin quantity control me. Baki 2 time diet plan follow karein.

3 Golden Rules Is Plan Ke

  1. Pani: Roz 3 litre pani lazmi. Weight loss 40% pani se hota hai.
  2. Walk: Roz 30 min walk. Gym zaroori nahi, ghar me stairs chadho.
  3. Neend: 7-8 ghante neend. Kam neend = weight loss ruk jata hai.

Result Kya Milega?

Agar ye plan 100% follow karein to 7 din me 3-5kg tak kam ho sakta hai. Pehle 2-3 din me 2kg water weight kam hoga, phir actual fat burn shuru.

Warning: Pregnancy, diabetes, ya koi bimari hai to doctor se mashwara kar lein. Ye general plan hai.

Agla Step Kya Hai?

7 din baad phir BMI check karein aur dekhein kitna farak para. Regular track karne ke liye hamara Age Calculator bhi use karein.
