CNIC Se Age Kaise Nikalein - 3 Asaan Tareeqay 2026

Published: April 23, 2026

Pakistan me har koi CNIC se apni age nikalna chahta hai. Job, admission, passport ke liye zaroorat parti hai. Tension na lein, 3 tareeqay hain:

1. Free Age Calculator Se

Sab se asaan tareeqa. Sirf DOB daalein aur age mil jayegi saal, mahine, din me.

→ Free Age Calculator Try Karein

2. SMS 8009 Se

Apna CNIC number bina dash ke likh kar 8009 pe SMS karein. 2 rupee lagega. Reply me age aa jayegi.

3. NADRA Website Se

NADRA ki website pe ja kar CNIC verify karein. Wahan DOB aur age dono nazar aayegi.

Tip: Sab se fast tareeqa hamara Age Calculator hai. 2 second me result.
