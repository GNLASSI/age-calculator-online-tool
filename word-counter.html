📖 What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in a given text. It is essential for writers, students, bloggers, and content creators who need to meet specific word count requirements or optimize their content.

Why Use This Tool?

  • 📝 Accurate Counting: Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs with precision.
  • ⏱️ Reading Time: Know how long it takes to read your content.
  • 🔑 Keyword Density: Optimize your content for SEO by analyzing keyword usage.
  • 🔒 Private: All processing is done in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
  • 📱 Mobile Friendly: Works on all devices.

🔒 Privacy: All processing is done locally in your browser. No text is uploaded to any server.
