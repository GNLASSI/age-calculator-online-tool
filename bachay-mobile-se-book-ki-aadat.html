Bachon ko Mobile ki Bajaye Kitaab ki Aadat Kaise Dalein? 8 Asaan Tarike

Sughar ho ya Karachi, har ghar ka masla: bacha mobile nahi chorta. Mobile se aankhein kharab, zehan sust. Lekin kitaab se bacha tez, confident banta hai. Ye 8 tarike azmaein:

1. Khud Model Banein

Bacha wahi karega jo aap karoge. Agar aap sara din mobile me hain to bacha bhi mobile maangega. Khud roz 15 min kitaab pakar ke baith jayein. Bacha dekh ke khud uth ke aayega.

2. Mobile ka Time Fix Karo

"Beta 1 ghanta mobile, phir 30 min kitaab" ka rule bana do. Timer lagao. Time khatam = mobile band. Rona dhona na suno, 3 din me adat ban jayegi.

3. Kitaab Interesting Banao

Bore wali kitaab mat do. Cartoon wali, kahaniyon wali, puzzle wali kitaab laao. Urdu ke "Iqbal ke parinde", "Phool ki kahani" bacho ko pasand ati hain.

4. Mobile ko "Inaam" Banao

Rule ye: "1 safha parho ge to 10 min mobile milega". Bacha mobile ke lalach me kitaab khud uthayega. Phir dheere dheere mobile kam, kitaab zyada.

5. Saath Baith ke Parho

Roz raat ko 15 min "kahani ka time" rakho. Bache ko god me bitha ke kahani sunao. Bacha mobile bhool jayega. Ye bonding bhi banayega.

6. Mobile se Door Jagah Banao

Ghar me 1 kona "Reading Corner" banao. Takiye, choti shelf, table lamp. Wahan mobile allowed nahi. Bacha wahan jaye ga to kitaab hi uthayega.

7. Inam Do, Maar Nahi

Bache ne 1 hafta rule follow kiya to chocolate, ice cream, ya park le jao. Daantne se bacha zid karega, inam se adat banegi.

8. School wali Kitaab se Shuru Karo

Bache ko Urdu ki kahani ya Science ka maza wala chapter parhao. Jo school me parh raha hai usi ko ghar dohrao. Bache ko lagega "main to top kar raha hun".

Important: 1 din me result na mango. 21 din lagte hain adat banne me. Sabar rakho. Mobile bilkul band mat karo warna bacha chup ke dekhega.

Bonus Tip: Age ke Hisab se Kitaab

5 saal ke bache ko 15 saal wali kitaab mat do. Age Calculator se bache ki exact age nikal ke us hisab se kitaab kharido.

Dua: Allah hamare bacho ko taleem wala, mobile ki addiction se mehfooz rakhe. Ameen
