📖 What is a Cover Letter?

A cover letter is a one-page document sent with your resume that introduces you to potential employers. It explains why you're interested in the position and why you're the best candidate for the job.

Why is it Important?

  • 🎯 First Impression: Your cover letter is often the first thing employers read.
  • 📝 Personalizes Your Application: It adds a personal touch to your resume.
  • 💪 Highlights Your Skills: It allows you to emphasize your most relevant skills.
  • 🚀 Shows Enthusiasm: It demonstrates your interest in the position.

Resume vs Cover Letter

  • Resume: A summary of your work experience, education, and skills.
  • Cover Letter: A personalized letter explaining why you're the ideal candidate.

ATS-Friendly Cover Letter Tips

  • ✅ Use standard fonts and formatting.
  • ✅ Include keywords from the job description.
  • ✅ Keep it concise (300-500 words).
  • ✅ Avoid images, tables, or fancy layouts.
  • ✅ Use a professional tone and language.
