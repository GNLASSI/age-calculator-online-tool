Tips for Choosing a Good Name

  • Keep it simple – Easy to spell and pronounce names are more memorable.
  • Make it meaningful – Choose a name that reflects your brand's values or story.
  • Check availability – Always check domain, social media, and trademark availability.
  • Think long-term – Choose a name that will grow with your brand.
  • Test it out – Say the name out loud and see how it feels.
