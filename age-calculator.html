📖 What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a simple tool that calculates the exact time elapsed since your birth. It shows your age in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds. It also provides additional insights like zodiac signs, next birthday countdown, and retirement date.

How to Calculate Age

Age is calculated by subtracting your date of birth from the current date or a specified date. The formula is:

Age = Current Date - Date of Birth

The result includes years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.
