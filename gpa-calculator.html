❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is GPA and how is it calculated? ▼

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the average of grade points earned per credit hour. It is calculated by:

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

Each subject has a grade (A, B, C, etc.) which corresponds to a grade point (4.0, 3.0, 2.0, etc.). Multiply the grade point by the credit hours to get the grade points for that subject.

What is CGPA and how is it different from GPA? ▼

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the overall GPA across all semesters.

CGPA = Total Grade Points Earned ÷ Total Credit Hours Completed

While GPA is calculated for a single semester, CGPA represents your entire academic performance.

What is a good GPA in Pakistani universities? ▼
  • 3.8 – 4.0: Excellent (Dean's List / Gold Medal)
  • 3.5 – 3.79: Very Good (Honors)
  • 3.0 – 3.49: Good
  • 2.5 – 2.99: Average
  • Below 2.5: Needs Improvement
Can I use this calculator for any university? ▼

Yes! Our GPA calculator supports multiple university standards including HITEC, Virtual University (VU), Punjab University, University of Peshawar, and Standard Pakistani system. You can also customize grade boundaries.

What GPA do I need to get a scholarship? ▼

Most scholarships require a minimum GPA of 3.0 or 3.5 depending on the scholarship type. Merit-based scholarships often require 3.5+, while need-based scholarships may have lower requirements. Use our Required GPA Calculator to determine exactly what GPA you need.
