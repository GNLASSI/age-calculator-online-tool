BMI 30+ Hai? 30 Din Me 6kg Kam Karne Ka Asaan Plan

Published: April 23, 2026

Pakistan me har 3 me se 1 banda overweight hai. BMI 30+ yani "Obese" category dil, diabetes ka risk barha deti hai. Par tension nahi - 30 din me 6kg tak kam kiya ja sakta hai.

Step 2: 30 Din Diet Plan

Subha: 2 boiled eggs + 1 roti + chai bina sugar
Dopahar: Salad + grilled chicken + 1 roti
Raat: Daal + sabzi + 1 roti
Band: Cheeni, cold drink, bakery items

Step 3: Roz 30 Min Walk

Khane ke baad 30 min walk zaroori hai. Weight tezi se kam hoga.
