Common Percentage Formulas

📐 Quick Formulas:
• Percentage of a number: (X × Y) ÷ 100
• What % one number is of another: (X ÷ Y) × 100
• Percentage increase: ((New - Original) ÷ Original) × 100
• Percentage decrease: ((Original - New) ÷ Original) × 100
