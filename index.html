What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a tool that computes your exact age based on your date of birth and the current date. Unlike simply subtracting years, a precise age calculator accounts for months, days, hours, and even minutes — giving you a complete picture of how long you've been alive.

This free online age calculator is useful for legal purposes, filling forms, curiosity, or planning birthday celebrations. It supports both 12-hour and 24-hour time formats and works for any date from the past century.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my exact age calculated?

We subtract your birth date and time from today's date and time, computing precise differences in years, months, days, hours, and minutes using calendar-accurate logic.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes! This tool is 100% free to use with no sign-up required. Simply enter your date of birth and click Calculate.

Can I calculate age for a past or future date?

Yes, you can set the "Calculate Age On" field to any date — past, present, or future — to see what your age was or will be on that date.

When is my next birthday?

After calculating, we show you exactly how many days remain until your next birthday.
