Why Word Count Matters for SEO

Search engines tend to favor content that thoroughly covers a topic, and word count is often a rough proxy for that. While there's no single "perfect" length, many well-ranking articles fall in a range that's long enough to add real value but not padded with filler. A word counter helps you track this without constantly switching between your writing tool and a separate calculator.

Word Count vs. Character Count — Why Both Matter

Word count is useful for essays and articles, but character count matters more for places with hard limits — like meta descriptions, SMS messages, or social media bios. Our tool tracks both at the same time, so you don't need a separate tool depending on what you're writing.
