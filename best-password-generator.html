Best Practices for Strong Passwords

Even when you use a password generator, it is smart to follow a few best practices. Use passwords that are long enough, preferably 12 characters or more. Longer passwords are harder to break and give better protection for sensitive accounts.

You should also avoid saving passwords in unsafe places like plain text notes or shared documents. A password manager is a better option because it stores your credentials securely. If possible, enable two-factor authentication on important accounts to add one more layer of protection.
