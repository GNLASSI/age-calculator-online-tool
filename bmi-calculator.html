📖 What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It is widely used to categorize individuals into weight categories that may indicate health risks.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)²

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese Class I: 30.0 – 34.9
  • Obese Class II: 35.0 – 39.9
  • Obese Class III: ≥ 40.0

Limitations of BMI

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic tool. It does not account for muscle mass, bone density, overall body composition, or distribution of fat. Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle, not fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for a complete assessment.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI? ▼

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight.

How is BMI calculated? ▼

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)². For imperial: BMI = (Weight in lbs / Height in inches²) × 703.

Is BMI accurate? ▼

BMI is a good screening tool but not a diagnostic tool. It may not be accurate for athletes, pregnant women, or elderly.

Is BMI different for men and women? ▼

BMI categories are the same for both sexes, but body fat distribution differs. Women generally have more body fat.

Can children use BMI? ▼

Yes, but BMI percentiles are used for children and teens based on age and gender.

What is a healthy BMI? ▼

A healthy BMI is between 18.5 and 24.9.

Can athletes have high BMI? ▼

Yes, athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass, not fat.

Is this calculator free? ▼

Yes, it is completely free to use.

Does BMI change with age? ▼

Body composition changes with age, but BMI categories remain the same for adults.

How do I improve my BMI? ▼

Eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

What is BMR? ▼

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body needs at rest.

What is ideal weight? ▼

Ideal weight is the weight range associated with the lowest health risk for your height.

What is body fat percentage? ▼

Body fat percentage is the proportion of fat to total body weight.

Does BMI work for elderly? ▼
