Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer really free?

Yes! Our online image resizer is 100% free forever. No registration, no watermark, no limits.

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No! All image processing happens entirely in your browser using client-side technology. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Can I resize images without losing quality?

Yes! Our advanced algorithm preserves image quality while resizing. You can also adjust the quality slider (1-100) to control compression levels.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, SVG, TIFF, HEIC, ICO, and more - over 10 formats in total.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes! Our batch processing feature allows you to resize multiple images simultaneously. Download all resized images as a ZIP file.

How do I crop an image?

Enable crop mode, then drag to select the area you want to keep. The image will be cropped to your selected dimensions.

Can I change image format after resizing?

Yes! Use the output format selector to convert your image to JPEG, PNG, WebP, or keep the original format.
