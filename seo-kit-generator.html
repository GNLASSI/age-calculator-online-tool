❓ Frequently Asked Questions (SEO)

What are meta tags and why are they important for SEO? ▼

Meta tags are HTML elements that provide information about your webpage to search engines and visitors. The most important meta tags include:

  • Title Tag – Appears in search results as the clickable headline. Keep it under 60 characters.
  • Meta Description – The short description that appears below your title in search results. Keep it under 160 characters.
  • Robots Tag – Tells search engines how to crawl your pages (index/follow).

Proper meta tags improve your click-through rate (CTR) and help search engines understand your content.

What is Schema Markup and how does it help SEO? ▼

Schema Markup (structured data) is code that helps search engines understand your content more deeply. Benefits include:

  • Rich Snippets – Star ratings, prices, and availability appear directly in search results.
  • Higher CTR – Rich snippets attract more clicks because they stand out.
  • Better Understanding – Google uses schema to grasp relationships between entities.
  • Voice Search – Structured data helps voice assistants find and read your content.

Common schema types include Article, Product, Recipe, FAQPage, and LocalBusiness.

What is Open Graph and why do I need it? ▼

Open Graph (OG) tags control how your content appears when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. Key benefits:

  • Control the title – What people see when sharing your content.
  • Control the description – The text that appears below the title.
  • Control the image – A high-quality image increases engagement by up to 10x.
  • Professional appearance – Your shared links look polished and trustworthy.

Without OG tags, social platforms will guess what to display—often with poor results.

What is Robots.txt and how do I use it? ▼

Robots.txt is a text file that tells search engine crawlers which parts of your website they can or cannot access. Key uses:

  • Block duplicate content – Prevent indexing of admin sections, staging environments, or filtered search results.
  • Save crawl budget – Focus search engines on your most important pages.
  • Submit sitemap – Reference your sitemap directly in robots.txt.
  • Set crawl-delay – Control how frequently crawlers access your site.

Always test your robots.txt using Google Search Console's robots.txt tester.

What is a Sitemap and why is it important? ▼

A sitemap is an XML file that lists all the important pages on your website. Benefits include:

  • Faster indexing – Search engines discover your new pages more quickly.
  • Better crawl efficiency – Helps search engines understand your site structure.
  • Index all pages – Ensures even deeply nested pages get indexed.
  • Provide metadata – Include last modification dates and priority levels.

Submit your sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for best results.

What is Breadcrumb Schema and how does it help? ▼

Breadcrumb Schema adds structured breadcrumb navigation to your search results. Benefits include:

  • Enhanced SERP appearance – Breadcrumbs appear below your title in search results.
  • Better user experience – Helps visitors understand where they are on your site.
  • Higher CTR – Breadcrumb-rich results stand out and attract more clicks.
  • Improved site structure – Search engines understand your site's hierarchy.

Breadcrumb markup is especially useful for e-commerce sites, blogs, and large websites with deep navigation.
