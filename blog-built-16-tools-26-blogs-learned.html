What I Learned About SEO

Building tools is one thing. Getting people to find them is another. Here's what I learned about SEO along the way [citation:2].

1. Google Search Console is Essential

I set up Google Search Console and submitted an XML sitemap early on [citation:2]. This helped me understand which pages were being indexed and which weren't. The Performance report shows which queries bring traffic – invaluable data.

📌 Pro Tip: Set up Google Search Console on day one. It's free and provides data no other tool can [citation:12].

2. Blog Posts Drive Organic Traffic

Creating SEO-focused blog posts has been one of the most effective strategies [citation:2]. Each blog post targets specific keywords and brings in targeted traffic. I've published 26+ posts so far, and they consistently drive visitors to the tools.

3. Internal Linking Matters

Linking between tools and blog posts helps with SEO and user experience [citation:1]. When someone reads a blog post about GPA calculation, they can easily find the GPA calculator tool.

4. Consistency Over Perfection

One of the biggest lessons: shipping consistently is more important than waiting for perfection [citation:2]. I've focused on releasing tools and posts regularly rather than polishing one thing forever.

⚠️ Lesson Learned: Don't wait for perfection. Launch, learn, and improve based on feedback.
